Storage & Ripening Tips

The Cold vs Counter Dilemma

Knowing which fruits ripen off the tree (climacteric) and which don’t (non-climacteric) helps you keep everything fresh and tasty.

Ripen on Counter (Climacteric)

  • Bananas
  • Avocados
  • Pears
  • Mangoes
  • Peaches & Plums
  • Kiwi

Refrigerate Immediately (Non-Climacteric)

  • Grapes
  • Strawberries & Berries
  • Cherries
  • Citrus Fruits
  • Pineapple

Ethylene Gas & Isolation

Some fruits produce ethylene gas, which speeds ripening. Keep ethylene-heavy fruits away from delicate fruits to prevent early spoilage.

Quick Tip

Need to ripen an avocado fast? Place it in a paper bag with an apple overnight. The trapped ethylene speeds up ripening like magic.

Washing Practices

Never wash berries or grapes until you are ready to eat them. Moisture speeds mold growth and kills shelf life instantly.
