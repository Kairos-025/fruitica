Shipping & Delivery

Cold-Chain Commitment

We use specialized, insulated packaging and high-density gel packs to maintain the cold chain from our facility to your door. This is critical to preserving the peak ripeness and flavor of your Frutica order.

Order Processing Times

  • Orders placed before 12:00 PM EST Monday–Friday typically ship the same day.
  • Orders placed after 12:00 PM EST or on weekends/holidays will be processed the next business day.
  • Due to the perishable nature of our products, we do not ship over the weekend to prevent packages from sitting in transit.

Important Delivery Note

Please ensure someone is available to receive the package upon delivery. As a perishable item, fruit should be refrigerated immediately. Frutica is not responsible for spoilage due to delivery delays caused by the recipient.
