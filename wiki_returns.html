Our Freshness Guarantee & Returns

The Frutica Peak Flavor Guarantee

We stand by our commitment that every piece of fruit is picked at its absolute peak. If your order arrives damaged, spoiled, or if the flavor simply does not meet your expectation for natural sweetness and quality, we will make it right.

We offer a replacement or full refund for all qualifying issues reported within 48 hours of delivery.

How to Report an Issue

To process your claim quickly, please follow these steps:

  1. Contact Us Immediately: Send an email to support@frutica.com within 48 hours of receiving your package.
  2. Provide Photos: Attach clear photos of the damaged or unsatisfactory fruit and the original packaging. This helps us file claims with our carriers and improve our packing methods.
  3. Include Order Details: Please include your Order Number, the specific product(s) affected, and a brief description of the issue.
Non-Qualifying Returns

Due to the nature of perishable goods, we cannot accept returns for the following reasons:

  • Recipient Error: Spoilage due to the recipient being unavailable to receive the package upon the first delivery attempt.
  • Improper Storage: Quality issues arising after 48 hours of delivery due to improper storage (e.g., leaving cold-chain items unrefrigerated).
  • Change of Mind: We cannot accept returns for simply changing your mind about an order.
Important

Please ensure that you report any delivery issues within 48 hours of receiving the package and include photos and order details. We cannot accept claims submitted after the 48-hour reporting window unless exceptional circumstances apply.
