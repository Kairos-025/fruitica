Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Frutica’s fruit “peak-ripened”?

Peak-ripened means your fruit is harvested at its natural flavor peak — not early for long storage. Fast cold-chain logistics preserve the exact state it was picked in, so it hits your door bursting with sweetness.

Do you offer organic or sustainably grown options?

Yup. We partner with farms practicing regenerative, organic, and ethically certified methods. Check product pages for the specific sourcing breakdown.

Can I track my order?

For sure. Once your order ships, you’ll get a tracking link. For shipping details, visit our Shipping & Delivery Wiki.

What if my fruit arrives damaged?

You’re covered with our Peak Flavor Guarantee. If your fruit arrives damaged, contact us within 48 hours with photos and we’ll replace or refund. Full policy is on the Returns & Guarantee Wiki.

How should I store my fruit?

Storage depends on the fruit. Some need room-temp ripening (like mangoes), others go straight to the fridge (like berries). Check the full chart at our Storage & Ripening Guide.

Do you offer wholesale or business order options?

Yep — we handle bulk supply for restaurants, cafés, offices, and corporate gifting. Check out our full breakdown here: Wholesale & Business Inquiries Wiki.
